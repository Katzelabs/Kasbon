In short:
  • KASBON stores your account details and everything you record about your business — products, sales, customer debts, and payment-proof photos.
  • Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in Singapore, readable only by your own account.
  • We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Payment-proof photos are deleted automatically once your chosen retention window passes (90 days by default).
  • You can request a copy of your data, or its deletion, at any time.

1. Who we are

KASBON (Kasir Bisnis Online) is a point-of-sale app for small and medium businesses in Indonesia. In this policy, "we" means the operator of the KASBON app, acting as the data controller for your account data and as a data processor for the customer data you enter into the app.

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@kasbon.app.

2. What we collect

Everything below comes from you. We do not buy data about you and we do not collect anything silently.

2.2 Your shop and business data

  • Shop profile: shop name, business type, address, phone number, logo, receipt header and footer text.
  • Catalogue: categories and products, including SKU, barcode, description, cost price, selling price, stock, and product photos.
  • Transactions: transaction number, date, line items, discounts, tax, total, payment method, cash received and change, notes, and cashier name.
  • Debts (kasbon): payment status and settlement date.
  • App settings: low-stock threshold, currency, and the payment-proof retention window.

2.3 Customer data you enter

When you record a sale or a debt you may enter a customer name. It is stored on that transaction and used to show your debt list and top-customer report. The app never asks for a customer's phone number, address, or national ID.

2.4 Photos

  • Product photos — taken with the camera or picked from the gallery, compressed, then stored in cloud storage.
  • Payment-proof photos — usually a photograph of a customer's phone screen showing a transfer or QRIS receipt. Such photos can contain another person's personal data, such as a name or part of a phone or account number. They are therefore kept in a private bucket that only the owning account can open, through short-lived signed links, and are deleted automatically at the end of the retention window (see section 6).

2.5 Device permissions

The app asks only for camera and photo library access, and only at the moment you actually take or choose a picture. It does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, or external storage. Denying camera access only disables the photo features; the rest of the app keeps working.

2.6 Technical data

  • Server logs. Our infrastructure provider (Supabase) keeps operational logs containing IP addresses, request times, and request types. These are used for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting — not to profile you.
  • On-device data. Your session token is kept in the device's secure storage and a few display preferences are stored locally. All of it disappears when you sign out or uninstall the app.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We show no advertising and send nothing to ad networks.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics SDK, tracker, or fingerprinting code.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not collect location, contact lists, or biometric data.
  • We do not process payments and never receive card numbers or bank credentials.

4. Purpose and legal basis

We process the data above to:

  • run the app — recording sales, calculating profit, tracking debts, and producing reports;
  • manage your account, including email verification and password recovery;
  • synchronise your data across your devices;
  • keep the service secure and prevent abuse;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

The legal basis is your consent given at registration and performance of the contract for the service, under Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP).

5. Where data is stored, and how it is protected

KASBON data is stored on Supabase infrastructure (PostgreSQL database and object storage) in the Singapore (ap-southeast-1) region. Your data is therefore stored and processed outside Indonesia; by using the app you consent to that transfer.

  • All traffic between the app and the server runs over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Every row belongs to one account and is protected by Row Level Security in the database itself, so one user cannot read another user's data — even if the app layer were to get something wrong.
  • The payment-proof bucket is private; access is only through short-lived signed links.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected users and the relevant authority as required by UU PDP.

8. Your responsibilities as a shop owner

Customer names and payment-proof photos are other people's personal data, entered by you. For that data you are the controller and we process it on your behalf. So:

  • enter a customer name only where you need one, for instance to track a debt;
  • ask the customer before photographing their payment proof;
  • delete payment-proof photos that are wrong or no longer needed, and set the retention window as short as your business allows;
  • forward any customer request about their data to us if you need help answering it.

9. Your rights

Under Law No. 27 of 2022 you have the right to:

  • know what data we process and why;
  • obtain a copy of your data — the backup export inside the app gives you all of your business data as JSON;
  • correct inaccurate data — most of it you can edit yourself in the app;
  • delete your data, in whole or in part (see section 10);
  • withdraw consent and stop using the service;
  • object or complain, to us or to the data protection authority.

Send requests to support@kasbon.app from the email address you registered with. We respond within 30 calendar days.

10. Deleting your account and data

There are two routes:

  • In the app. Open Settings → Account → Delete Account and confirm.
  • By email. Send a deletion request to support@kasbon.app from your account's email address. We process it within 30 calendar days.

Deleting your account removes the profile, shop settings, categories, products, transactions and their line items, debt records, product photos, and payment-proof photos belonging to that account. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — export a backup first if you still need the data. Copies held in our infrastructure provider's system backups age out on their own backup cycle.

You can also delete parts of your data without closing your account: delete products, remove the payment proof on a specific transaction, or shorten the payment-proof retention window.

11. Children

KASBON is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe that has happened, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version. For changes that materially affect your data, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
