Deleting Your KASBON Account

App: KASBON — Kasir Bisnis Online · Updated 4 August 2026

Baca halaman ini dalam Bahasa Indonesia

In short:
  • You can delete your own account from inside the app, without contacting anyone.
  • Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no grace period and no undo.
  • All of your business data goes with it, including product photos and payment-proof photos.
  • Export a backup first if you still need the data.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open KASBON and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the Pengaturan (Settings) tab.
  3. Scroll to the Akun (Account) section and tap Hapus Akun (Delete Account).
  4. Read the list of what will be deleted. If you still need the data, tap Buat Backup Dulu (Back Up First) to download everything as a single JSON file, then come back to this step.
  5. Enter your account password and type HAPUS in the confirmation field.
  6. Tap Hapus Akun. The account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

The password is asked for because a point-of-sale device usually sits signed in on a shop counter. Without it, anyone holding your device could destroy your entire business record.

Option 2 — By email

If you can no longer sign in — a lost device, or a forgotten password with recovery mail not arriving — send a deletion request to kasbon@katzeapps.com from your account's email address, including:

  • the email address of the account to delete;
  • the registered shop name, as an additional check.

Requests are processed within 30 calendar days. We only accept requests sent from the account's own address, because there is no other way to confirm the requester owns the account.

What may briefly remain

  • Infrastructure backups. Copies inside Supabase's automated backups age out on their own backup cycle. They are not reachable from the app.
  • Technical logs. Server logs that contain no business data may be kept briefly for security and diagnostics.
  • Files you downloaded yourself. JSON backups and receipts saved on your own device are yours and are untouched by this deletion.

Deleting some data without closing the account

If you only want to clear part of your data, the account does not have to go:

  • delete individual products or categories from their lists;
  • delete the payment-proof photo on a specific transaction;
  • shorten the payment-proof retention window in Pengaturan → Pengaturan Aplikasi (default 90 days, adjustable from 7 days to 10 years);
  • use Pengaturan → Backup & Restore → Hapus Semua Data to empty all business data while keeping the account.
